OLEA / NAVIGATION STUDY 03

Two ways to move between workspaces and conversations

Both start here at conversation history for comparison—not the app's launch screen. Tap a workspace, open a chat, and navigate back. Files are only in the chat's ⋯ menu.

A · Parallel destinations

Conversations is a global list. Choosing a workspace switches to that list, filtered to the workspace. Tabs remain available.

Workspaces tab → select Olea → Conversations tab (Olea filter)

B · In and out

Workspaces contain conversation lists. Go out to workspaces, then into one workspace's conversations. No parallel tabs.

Workspaces → Olea conversations → Chat
