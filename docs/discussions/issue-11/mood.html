Three proposals for a new flat design

These are original browser design specimens, not Android screenshots or approved product changes. The same content and three screens appear in every direction.

All three reduce repetitive outlines, group agent actions with their agent, remove the global new-chat action from management screens, simplify navigation, and give conversation content more emphasis.

The model control, provider presets, advanced-settings disclosure, formatted replies and management navigation are proposed interactions. Their sample appearance does not establish adapter support or settle behavior.

Sample installed, connected and completed states are illustrative. Fonts use available system families; production font choice, accessibility, native layout and motion need validation.

Recommendation: Clear, for its compact controls and strong reading hierarchy. Soft gives more space and warmth. Field gives more density and a tool-oriented character.
