## A — Seed

**AGENT PLAN:** A single almond-shaped form, opened by a diagonal seam, establishes a small, concentrated center of gravity. The subtle reference is an olive seed or leaf; it need not be read literally. The mark should feel tangible and recognizable before any explanation. Its asymmetric contour receives careful optical adjustment rather than mechanical symmetry.

Warm ivory and deep olive establish the material atmosphere. Pale chartreuse appears sparingly as a bright interior surface. Flat color and crisp edges carry the identity; texture is unnecessary. Careful spacing between the two halves matters more than decorative detail.

The wordmark uses a restrained sans serif. Large fields of empty space balance an enlarged, cropped seed in banners and posters. The same form provides rhythm through changes of scale, with painstaking attention to where the seam meets each edge.

Composition stays quiet and direct. One mark, one name and at most one short descriptor make the hierarchy. The intended feeling is calm agency and a tool worth keeping close. This is the recommended starting direction because it connects the name and current reference palette. **OPEN:** A botanical reading could suggest wellness or gardening; refinement should test this association.

## B — Aperture

**AGENT PLAN:** A geometric O opens toward a short horizontal cursor. The subtle reference combines the initial letter with an active command surface. Circular mass and a deliberate interruption create recognition without a literal phone, robot or chat bubble. The opening must remain clear when reduced.

Ink blue, cool white and a concentrated electric blue create a technical atmosphere. A single bright accent carries the energy. Precision comes from the relationship between ring thickness, opening and cursor length, with each junction optically balanced.

The wordmark is geometric and compact. Large rings extend beyond the edges of banners; smaller cursor bars establish a measured horizontal rhythm. Posters can use one oversized aperture and ample blank space, with the name aligned to its opening.

The hierarchy gives the symbol first attention and keeps supporting text short. Careful construction should produce a clear, purposeful instrument. **OPEN:** Geometric O marks are common; this direction requires substantial distinctiveness work and a later similarity review before adoption.

## C — Fold

**AGENT PLAN:** A continuous-looking folded ribbon builds a compact, angular loop. Its subtle reference is a portable working surface folded into a small object. Different planar faces give the mark its structure. The intersections require meticulous attention so the symbol remains legible as a silhouette.

Dark aubergine, warm lilac and pale peach establish a more expressive editorial identity. Light and dark faces describe a fold without gradients. The palette can stand alongside the quiet app reference without deciding the app's Material theme.

A light sans serif wordmark leaves the ribbon as the main gesture. Banner compositions use enlarged diagonal planes, while posters can isolate a monumental fold against generous pale space. The same angles determine cropping and alignment.

The composition should feel independent and slightly playful. Sparse text and precisely controlled diagonals preserve clarity. **OPEN:** This is the largest visual departure from the current olive reference, and the folded-loop family also needs a later similarity review.
