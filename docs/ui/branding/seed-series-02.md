## Visual direction

**AGENT PLAN:** Extend the selected Seed identity through scale, placement and the balance of pale and deep olive. Preserve the original leaf contour and diagonal seam. The seed is the visual signature; the compositions change around it. Careful optical spacing and the relationship of each leaf tip to the canvas edges establish continuity.

Dark fields give the portrait poster and thin banner a concentrated presence. Light fields give the landscape and square images a quieter editorial character. The palette remains the selected deep olive, pale olive and warm ivory. Flat color and precisely rendered contours supply the material quality without texture or effects.

Typography has two roles: Outfit for the Olea name and Instrument Sans for the slogan. The approved wording remains exact, including its comma and final period; line breaks adapt to the format. Each composition uses generous margins and deliberate line spacing. There is no added feature list, agent logo, product screenshot or performance claim.

The portrait balances an isolated, monumental seed above a large left-aligned slogan. The landscape contrasts a pale text field with a dark cropped-symbol panel. The thin banner arranges the name, slogan and symbol horizontally. The square uses a centered emblem and centered slogan. This is a coordinated set of compositions rather than four crops of one image.
